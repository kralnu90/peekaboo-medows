Make the talking voice sound more natural

The talking voice comes from your phone or tablet. Most devices can download better, more human-sounding voices for free. Download one, then come back and pick it under Voice above.

Android

  1. Open Settings and search for Text-to-speech.
  2. Make sure the preferred engine is Speech Services by Google.
  3. Tap the gear next to it, then Install voice data.
  4. Choose English (United Kingdom), pick a voice you like and let it download.
  5. Come back here, reload the game and choose the new voice.

iPhone and iPad

  1. Open Settings, then Accessibility, then Spoken Content, then Voices.
  2. Tap English, then pick a voice marked Enhanced or Premium and download it.
  3. Come back here, reload the game and choose the new voice.

Wording of menus varies a little between phone makers. If a voice doesn't show up, restart the browser and try again.

Record your own voice

Tap Record, say the line, then tap Stop (it stops by itself after 4 seconds). Tap Play to hear it back. Anything you don't record uses the device voice instead. Recordings stay on this device only.
